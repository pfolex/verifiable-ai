# Initial AIX governance proposal

The repository owner remains the decision maker until additional maintainers explicitly agree to documented responsibilities. No board, advisory group, or review capacity is assumed to exist.

- Contributors propose and implement scoped work voluntarily.
- Workstream coordinators opt into triage and dependency maintenance; vacancies stay visible.
- Reviewers check implementation and evidence within their competence.
- Independent reproducers confirm headline claims using their own recorded environment.
- Release approval requires a consenting maintainer and evidence linked to an immutable artifact.

Research proposals state a hypothesis, controls, validation budget, stop rule, rights/provenance, and measurable outcome. Negative results are valid. Priorities change through a public decision record explaining the evidence and affected dependencies. A large backlog is not a staffing commitment or a guaranteed delivery schedule.

Workers cannot approve their own results, change release catalogs, or receive administrative access just for donating compute. Access follows least privilege and explicit consent. Conduct reporting, incident response, succession, and formal decision rules are dedicated roadmap tasks; this document does not claim those processes are already staffed.
